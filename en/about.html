Our Story

Since 2004,
At Every Stage.

From initial site surveys and licensing to public tenders, from our own power plants to international asset transfers — we have directly managed the full lifecycle of energy. Not theoretical, but field-verified.

2004 2010

Run-of-River HPP Development and Portfolio Advisory

Using our own equity and engineering capacity, we successfully developed and sold 9 hydroelectric power plants (HPPs) totalling 80 MW of installed capacity from scratch. During the same period, we conducted technical reviews, feasibility studies and investment advisory for nearly 480 MW of HPP projects across Turkey.

2007 2015

Wind Energy (WPP) Development and Capacity Tenders

Through wind measurement masts erected across Turkey, a major WPP project portfolio of 295.6 MW was developed. In the TEİAŞ capacity allocation tenders held in 2011, we directly won 74.2 MW of licensed wind capacity through competitive bidding processes.

2007 2016

International Carbon Certification — South Pole Partnership

A strategic partnership was established with South Pole, the global leader in climate and carbon management. International carbon registrations were completed for the Çaldere HPP (8.91 MWe — VCS), Dora-1 JES (7.36 MWe — VCS) and Dora-2 JES (9.50 MWe — Gold Standard) facilities. Verification and emission reduction documentation processes were maintained for 9 years.

2010

Public Privatisation Tenders (POI) and Site Valuation

In the public run-of-river HPP privatisations conducted by the Privatisation Administration (POI), we directly managed the technical site inspection, valuation reporting and tender bid processes for plants across 3 portfolio groups (Groups 7, 11 and 14).

2016 2020

Solar EPC, Own-Account Installations and Facility Inspections

As solar energy entered the market, we reached 220 MW+ of engineering, EPC, own-account investment and independent inspection volume across utility-scale and rooftop solar projects. IEC-standard quality assurance was applied, including Thermal Drone, I-V Curve and EL testing.

2021 2026

BESS Integration, M&A Brokerage and Regional Expansion

Focus shifted to Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) and hybrid plant engineering. With the contribution of 20 years of developer experience and specialist energy lawyers, Project Brokerage & M&A became a standalone business line. Regional opportunities in Eastern Europe and EU markets are actively monitored.

Developing, operating or transferring a project means correctly reading not just today's numbers, but the balance of risk and opportunity at every moment across the 8,760 hours of the year. This is why we placed 'Engineering Precision' at the heart of our philosophy. We believe not in paper promises, but in field-verified data and legal certainty.
