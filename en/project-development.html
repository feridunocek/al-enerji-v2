Development & EPC

From Concept to Commercial Operation:
7-Stage End-to-End Project Development

The financial success of a renewable energy power plant depends on the accuracy of site analyses, land selection and administrative permitting decisions made at the concept stage. We manage every step — from grid connection capacity research to Industrial Registry Certificate — under one roof.

Site & Regulatory Road Map

Detailed 7-Stage Project Development Process

Full regulatory compliance with Turkish renewable energy legislation — complete chain of responsibility through to Ministry acceptance. Backed by 1 GW+ of portfolio experience in the field.

01Step 01

Capacity Research, Site Selection & Land Pre-Analysis

Investigation of grid connection capacity and determination of the land's legal status. The most critical first step influencing the entire project lifecycle.

  • Grid Capacity Assessment: Analysis of available and restricted connection capacities at TEİAŞ and TEİAŞ/TEDAŞ/EDAŞ substations
  • Land Ownership & Legal Status: Review of Treasury, Pasture, Forest or private ownership conditions
  • Allocation & Expropriation Feasibility: Preliminary assessment of leasing, purchase, easement and land-use reclassification options
TEİAŞ CapacityLand OwnershipLand-Use Reclassification
02Step 02

Capacity Allocation & Tender / Application Management

Preparation of licensed and unlicensed generation applications and management of capacity tender processes.

  • Licensed & Unlicensed Application Files: Technical and administrative dossiers compliant with EMRA (EPDK) Unlicensed Electricity Generation Regulation (Articles 5.1.h, 5.1.c) and RES legislation
  • Competition & Tender Processes: TEİAŞ capacity competitions, Privatisation Administration (ÖİB) tenders, YEKA (Renewable Energy Resource Areas) competitions
  • Grid Connection Agreement & Call Letter: Procurement of a call letter or grid connection agreement from the relevant authority
EPDK ApplicationYEKACall LetterConnection Agreement
03Step 03

Site Surveys, Measurements & Pre-Construction

Topographic and geological surveys on site. Installation of TSMS (Turkish State Meteorological Service)-approved wind measurement infrastructure for WPP projects.

  • Topographic Map & Geology: Site topographic surveys, geological and geotechnical study reports required for zoning plans
  • Wind & Solar Measurement Stations: TSMS-approved wind measurement mast installation with at least 12 months of continuous data collection; irradiation analysis for SPP projects
Topographic SurveyGeotechnical StudyWind MeasurementTSMS
04Step 04

EIA Process, Urban Planning & Regulatory Approvals

Environmental Impact Assessment and zoning plan preparation. Collection of positive opinions from 20+ public authorities.

  • EIA Process Management: EIA application file, public participation meetings and obtaining the "EIA Positive" decision
  • Zoning Plans: Preparation and approval of Master Development Plan (1:5,000) and Implementation Development Plan (1:1,000)
  • 20+ Public Authority Opinions: Obtaining opinions from DSI (State Hydraulic Works), Highways, TEİAŞ, Cultural Heritage, Provincial Agriculture, National Defence and other authorities
  • Land Conservation Board: Agricultural suitability reports under Law No. 5403
EIA Positive1:5,000 Zoning1:1,000 ZoningLand Conservation
05Step 05

Detailed Engineering Design, Simulations & Technical Approvals

Preparation of production simulations and electromechanical designs, followed by obtaining official project approvals.

  • Production Simulations: 3D shading and hourly generation analysis using PVSyst (SPP), WAsP/WindPro (WPP)
  • Electromechanical Design: Substation, LV/MV cabling, inverter and turbine layouts, single-line diagrams
  • Official Project Approvals: Formal approval of engineering projects at MENR (ETKB) or TEİAŞ/TEDAŞ
PVSystWAsP/WindProLV/MV DesignMENR Approval
06Step 06

Licensing, Equipment Procurement & EPC Construction Management

Obtaining the construction permit and completing Tier-1 equipment procurement along with site assembly.

  • Construction Permit & Licences: Procurement of the construction permit in line with approved zoning plans and electrical/structural projects
  • Site Infrastructure: Access roads to site, WPP heavy transport routes, earthworks and ground improvement
  • Procurement & Assembly: Tier-1 equipment procurement tracking, logistics, construction piling, cabling and mechanical installation
Construction PermitTier-1 EquipmentSite Assembly
07Step 07

Official Ministry Acceptance, Commercial Operation & Industrial Registry

Legal completion of the project — final steps from MENR (ETKB) acceptance through to the Industrial Registry Certificate.

  • Official MENR Acceptance: Formal acceptance and minutes by the Ministry of Energy and Natural Resources (ETKB) Acceptance Commission
  • Commercial Operation (COD) & Occupancy Permit: Building Use Permit and Workplace Opening Licence
  • Industrial Registry: Registration in the Ministry of Industry and Technology system and acquisition of the Industrial Registry Certificate
MENR AcceptanceCODOccupancy PermitIndustrial Registry Certificate

Frequently Asked Questions

About Project Development

How is land-use reclassification carried out when developing an SPP or WPP project on pasture land?+
Developing a project on pasture land requires an application to the Pasture Commission at the Provincial Directorate of Agriculture and Forestry. Once the investment is documented as serving the public interest, the commission review is completed. After the grazing fee is deposited with the administration, the pasture designation is changed to suit the intended use, subject to Governorship approval. Depending on regional workload, this process typically takes 4–8 months.
Is obtaining an Industrial Registry Certificate mandatory for unlicensed SPP/WPP facilities after Ministry acceptance?+
Yes. Under Industrial Registry Law No. 6948, facilities engaged in electricity generation have the status of an industrial enterprise. After formal Ministry acceptance, it is a legal requirement to register in the Ministry of Industry and Technology system within 2 months, obtain the Industrial Registry Certificate and submit annual operating schedules every year.
Does AL ENERJİ provide project development and advisory services outside Turkey?+
With the aim of extending our 1 GW+ project development experience in Turkey into international markets, we actively analyse land, grid and permitting processes in Eastern Europe and regional markets in particular. All our engineering designs are structured in compliance with IEC standards and European Grid Codes (EU Grid Codes).
Why is the installation of a wind measurement mast and continuous data monitoring mandatory for WPP projects?+
Measurement masts installed in accordance with TSMS and international standards record wind speed, direction and turbulence intensity at the site without interruption for at least 12 months. This data is vital both to prove the project's productivity in EMRA licence applications and bank financing, and to select the correct turbine type.
