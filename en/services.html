Brokerage & Advisory

Project Brokerage, M&A and Contract Advisory

Unlike traditional financial intermediary models, our M&A and Brokerage service is grounded in over 20 years of field development, EPC and directly executed project sales experience. Together with specialist energy lawyers, we manage a comprehensive transfer process that eliminates technical, financial and contractual risks for both buyers and sellers.

  • Contract Engineering: Conducting technical and commercial negotiations for LOI / Term Sheets, SPAs and SHAs
  • Condition Precedent & Risk Structuring: Complete structuring of security arrangements, Set-off and Escrow mechanisms
  • Realistic Asset Valuation: DCF modelling based on hourly spot-market simulations and actual historical costs

Development & EPC

End-to-End Project Development (Concept to Commercial Operation)

We do not leave projects on paper — we manage every process from concept to Industrial Registry Certificate acquisition with a chronological discipline that maps precisely to the realities of regulations and fieldwork. 7-phase site management fully compliant with Turkey's renewable energy legislation.

  • Capacity Research & Site Selection: Identification of TEİAŞ/TEDAŞ transformer substation capacities; land lease, purchase or expropriation analysis
  • EIA & Urban Planning: Preparation of 1/5,000 and 1/1,000 zoning plans with urban planners, collection of all public authority opinions
  • Engineering Design & Simulations: PVSyst / WAsP production simulations, 3D shading analyses, electrical-mechanical implementation projects
  • Official Acceptance & Commercial Operation: MENR ministerial acceptance, COD, occupancy permit and Industrial Registry Certificate acquisition

03
Storage

Battery Energy Storage (BESS) & Hybrid Plant Solutions

We overcome the intermittent nature of renewable energy through Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) and hybrid plant conversion engineering, maximising facilities' market revenues. LFP cell chemistry, BMS integration and full compliance with TEİAŞ grid codes.

  • Optimum BESS Capacity & C-Rate Analysis: MWh and C-rate selection matched to historical production profiles, grid constraints and spot market price volatility
  • Advanced Battery Technology Integration: LFP cell chemistry, container architecture, BMS and fire suppression infrastructure
  • Hybrid Plant Conversion Engineering: Addition of secondary resources to existing HPP, WPP or SPP facilities to utilise licensed capacity at full efficiency
  • Grid Codes & Ancillary Services: 100% technical compliance with TEİAŞ Primary/Secondary Frequency Control and Reactive Power Support standards

04
Development & EPC

EPC & Turnkey Installation (Rooftop & Ground-Mount SPP)

We deliver engineering, procurement and construction services for rooftop and ground-mount Solar Power Plants (SPPs) in compliance with IEC 62446, IEC 61724 and UL standards. Single point of accountability from design through commissioning tests.

  • Design & Engineering Optimisation: PVSyst simulations, structural and load-bearing calculations, MV/LV single-line diagrams
  • DC/AC Ratio Optimisation (Overplanting): Determination of overplanting ratios to utilise inverter capacity at maximum efficiency
  • Tier-1 Equipment Procurement: High-efficiency module/inverter selection, driven/bored piling, structural assembly and transformer substation installation
  • Commissioning & Acceptance Tests: Acceptance testing and performance reporting in accordance with IEC standards

Brokerage & Advisory

Site Inspection, Survey & Testing (Owner's Engineer)

We provide independent Owner's Engineering (OE) and Independent Technical Review (Independent Engineering – IE) on behalf of investors, international funds and banks during the construction, acceptance and operations phases of power plants.

  • Field Quality Inspection: Real-time on-site monitoring of the EPC contractor's workmanship quality and programme on behalf of the investor
  • FAT & SAT Test Consultancy: Independent witnessing of equipment performance acceptance tests at factory (FAT) and on-site (SAT)
  • Plant Health Check & PR Analysis: Hotspot detection via thermal drone, I-V Curve measurements and EL micro-crack testing
  • Performance Ratio (PR) Verification Reporting: Independent technical reporting in compliance with international standards
