Isparta Gönen SPP

A project archive using aerial and close field photography to show plant layout, topography and solar arrays together.

Project Overview

A solar project illustrating how panel fields respond to the natural form of the site.

Located in Gönen, Isparta, the project is a ground-mounted solar power plant with 2.25 MWp DC and 2 MWe AC installed capacity. Aerial views reveal the distribution of plant blocks across the terrain and their relationship with the service roads.

Closer field photographs capture the panel rows, mounting system and surrounding landscape. Together, the images provide a clear reading of both the overall layout and the implementation character.
