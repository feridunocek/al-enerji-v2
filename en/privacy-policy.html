Privacy Policy

A plain-language explanation of how information is handled when you use our website.

This policy explains AL ENERJİ's privacy practices for its corporate website. For the detailed processing conditions under Türkiye's KVKK, please also see our Privacy Notice.

1. Scope

This policy applies when you visit the website, use the contact form or contact AL ENERJİ through an email address or phone number displayed here. Links to other websites are governed by those third parties' own privacy practices.

2. Information collected

  • Information you provide: name, company, email, optional phone, project location and capacity, subject, message and NDA preference.
  • Technical information: IP address, access time, browser/device, pages requested, referring page and security/error records.
  • Communications: enquiries sent by email or phone and our responses.

The contact form's backend submission system is not yet active. When activated, the provider and data flow will be reflected in this policy and the Privacy Notice.

3. How we use information

  • To assess and respond to project, proposal and partnership enquiries,
  • to route an enquiry to the appropriate specialist team,
  • to operate, secure and maintain the website,
  • to diagnose technical issues and prevent misuse,
  • to comply with legal obligations and protect legal rights.

We do not sell personal data for advertising. Contact details submitted through this website are not used for electronic marketing unless separate permission or another valid legal basis applies.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

AL ENERJİ's website does not currently use advertising, retargeting or visitor-profiling cookies. Hosting and security infrastructure may use strictly necessary technical identifiers or short-lived security records to deliver the site and prevent misuse.

If analytics or preference cookies are introduced, an appropriate notice and preference mechanism will be provided where required, and this policy will be updated.

5. Third-party services and links

The site may use technical providers for hosting and content delivery, Google Fonts for web typography, and Unsplash for certain images. Loading these resources may disclose standard connection data—such as IP address, browser information and request time—to the relevant provider.

The Google Maps link opens the mapping service in a new tab; no map is embedded on this website. A third party's privacy policy applies after you open an external link.

6. Security and retention

We apply technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the purpose to protect data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss. No internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely risk-free.

Information is retained for the period necessary for its purpose and applicable statutory retention or limitation periods, after which it is deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

7. Your rights and contact

Your rights of access, information, correction, deletion, objection and compensation under the KVKK, together with the application procedure, are explained in the Privacy Notice.
