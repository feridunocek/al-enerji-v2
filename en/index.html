End-to-End Project Development

Engineering Precision:
From Concept to Commercial Operation

We do not merely broker projects — in our own plants and investor projects, we directly manage every process from the first capacity study through commercial operation and official registrations, following the true chronology of regulations and fieldwork:

01 / 07

Capacity Research, Site Selection & Land Pre-Analysis

01 / 07

Capacity Research, Site Selection & Land Pre-Analysis

Research into grid connection capacity (TEİAŞ/TEDAŞ); identification of suitable land parcels; analysis of feasibility for lease, purchase or expropriation. Investigation of land status and preliminary land-use reclassification studies.

02 / 07

Capacity Allocation & Tender / Application Processes

Submission of capacity allocation applications appropriate to the plant and activity type; monitoring and management of competitive tender or direct criterion-based allocation processes.

03 / 07

Site Surveys, Measurements & Pre-Construction

Following capacity allocation: acquisition and approval of survey maps for zoning plans; geological and geotechnical surveys; execution of regulatory wind/irradiation measurement processes for wind and solar projects; preparation of Land Conservation Board reports.

04 / 07

EIA Process, Urban Planning & Regulatory Approvals

Management of Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) processes and obtaining decisions. Preparation of zoning plans with urban planners, submission, collection of all public authority opinions; completion of public display and approval processes for zoning plans with positive EIA and authority opinions.

05 / 07

Engineering Design, Simulations & Technical Approvals

Detailed electrical and mechanical design in parallel with zoning and EIA processes, preparation of production simulations, and obtaining technical project approvals from relevant public/grid authorities.

06 / 07

Licensing, Equipment Procurement & EPC Construction

Obtaining construction permits; site preparation and road analysis/infrastructure works for wind projects. Finalising procurement agreements for major equipment (turbine, panel, inverter, BESS), logistics tracking, and full execution of construction and site assembly.

07 / 07

Official Acceptance, Commercial Operation & Industrial Registry

Official Ministry acceptance, commissioning to commercial operation; obtaining occupancy, workplace opening and operating permits. Industrial registry during commercial operation, acquisition of Industrial Registry Certificate and regular monitoring of production data.
