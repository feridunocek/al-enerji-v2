Rooftop & Ground-Mounted SPP EPC Services

Reaching the targeted annual generation values in solar power plants is not possible simply by purchasing quality equipment. With our 220 MW+ EPC portfolio, we are your single authorised point of contact from engineering design through commissioning tests.

EPC Implementation Architecture

Detailed 4-Phase EPC Process

Turnkey installation fully compliant with IEC standards, under the supervision of our field engineers, from engineering design through official Ministry acceptance.

01Phase 01

Detailed Engineering, Design & Structural Analysis

The project's generation potential and the mechanical limits of the site or roof are determined through comprehensive engineering analyses.

  • 3D Shadow & PVSyst Simulation: Shading losses caused by chimneys, parapets or topography are simulated using a 3D digital twin
  • Roof Structural & Wind/Snow Load Calculations: The additional load-bearing capacity of existing roof trusses and cladding is analysed using structural software
  • Electrical Single-Line & Layout Design: DC cable cross-section optimisation, string configuration, inverter placement, LV/MV transformer substation designs

02Phase 02

Tier-1 Equipment Procurement & Logistics Management

Selection and management of procurement processes for durable equipment that meets international financing requirements.

  • Tier-1 Module Procurement: Procurement agreements for high-efficiency TOPCon, HJT or Bifacial photovoltaic modules
  • Inverter & MV Transformer Selection: String or central inverter optimisation and unit selection
  • Mounting & Corrosion Resistance: Ground-appropriate driven/concrete systems for land installations; corrosion-resistant aluminium/stainless mounting for rooftops

03Phase 03

Site Assembly, Construction & Health & Safety

Plant installation is carried out under the supervision of our field engineers, in full compliance with IEC and HSE standards.

  • Site Preparation & Earthworks: Ground levelling, security infrastructure, excavation of cable ducts
  • Mechanical & Electrical Assembly: Structural mounting installation, panel layout, MC4 connector wiring, DC/LV cabling and earthing
  • HSE & Quality Management: Working-at-height and LV/MV electrical safety protocols in accordance with international HSE standards

Testing, Commissioning, Acceptance & Occupancy Permit

The plant is brought to commercial production readiness. All acceptance tests in accordance with IEC standards are completed.

  • IEC 62446 Commissioning: Insulation resistance tests, string Voc and Isc measurements, polarity checks
  • Thermal Camera & I-V Curve Tests: Thermal drone inspections at panel junction points and cell level
  • Official Ministry Acceptance & Occupancy Permit: TEDAŞ/ETKB acceptance committee process, acceptance certificate and building occupancy permit

Frequently Asked Questions

About EPC & Installation

How is structural suitability verified for rooftop SPP projects?+
Prior to the project, the existing architectural and structural drawings of the factory or building are reviewed. Structural elements are inspected on-site to verify that the additional load of the panels and mounting structure (approximately 12–18 kg per m²) is compliant with the building's earthquake, snow and wind load tolerances, confirmed through university-approved structural reports.
Why is the DC/AC ratio (Overplanting) important in solar power plants?+
Solar panels deliver their nominal power only under ideal laboratory conditions (STC). Under real field conditions, power losses occur due to temperature and low irradiance. By installing DC panel capacity 20% to 50% above inverter capacity (e.g. a 1.3–1.5 DC/AC ratio), inverters are kept operating at full capacity throughout the day, maximising annual generation output.
Does AL ENERJİ provide SPP EPC and project development services in European markets?+
Building on our 220 MW+ SPP engineering and EPC experience in Turkey, we actively monitor renewable energy investments and EPC opportunities particularly in Eastern Europe and EU countries. All our engineering designs, equipment supply chains and testing procedures are structured in compliance with international IEC standards and EU Grid Codes.
What does the IEC 62446 standard mean in solar power plant installation?+
IEC 62446 is the international standard for the documentation, acceptance testing and inspection of grid-connected photovoltaic systems. Reporting DC earthing continuity, polarity, string open-circuit voltage (Voc), insulation resistance and operating current tests in accordance with this standard during commissioning confirms the safety of the plant.
